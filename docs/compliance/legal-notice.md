# Legal & Regulatory Notice — Australian Market

## 1. Compliance Notice

This platform and its codebase constitute an **engineering and software development implementation**. It is **not legal advice** and does not establish that real-money online rummy is licensed or legally operational in Australia without formal regulatory approval.

---

## 2. Regulatory Framework (Interactive Gambling Act 2001)

- Australia's **Interactive Gambling Act 2001 (IGA)** regulates interactive gambling services offered to persons physically present in Australia.
- The **Australian Communications and Media Authority (ACMA)** actively investigates, regulates, and requests DNS blocking of prohibited and unapproved interactive gambling services.
- Real-money gaming offerings require explicit legal review by an Australian licensed gaming compliance practitioner.

---

> [!CAUTION]
